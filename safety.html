Safety, Legal Protection & Platform Policies

Thorough operational frameworks, rigorous liability limitations, consumer protection protocols, and frequently addressed inquiries governing the Taskly ecosystem.

Standard Safety & Risk Mitigation Guidelines

1. Independent Verification & Credential Auditing

NaijaFix operates strictly as an independent digital directory aggregator connecting service seekers with independent trade professionals. While our administrative desk conducts preliminary reviews and awards "Verified" tags to established operators, users bear sole responsibility for independently verifying professional licenses, certifications, trade references, and identity credentials before granting job access or executing contracts.

2. Financial Milestone & Escrow Advisory

To safeguard against financial exposure or fraudulent transactions, users are strongly advised never to disburse full prepayments or unverified cash advances upfront. All financial terms must be organized across clear, incremental project milestones, ensuring final monetary settlement only after rigorous personal inspection and verification of completed service deliverables.

3. Physical Security & On-Site Consultation Protocols

When inviting independent trade practitioners to residential or commercial locations for evaluations or repairs, clients must ensure a secondary witness or representative is present. For movable equipment or high-value repair items, handovers should preferably occur in secure, well-lit, public environments.

4. Maintenance of Official Communication Trails

To protect both parties against disputes, miscommunications, or contract breaches, all service discussions, pricing quotations, timeline agreements, and scope modifications must be explicitly documented and retained through official NaijaFix communication conduits and verified direct WhatsApp links.

Privacy Policy & Legal Disclaimer

1. Data Acquisition & Information Governance

NaijaFix harvests and processes structured user data voluntarily submitted during onboarding or directory engagement, encompassing trade business names, operational categories, regional location vectors, and WhatsApp contact numbers. Technical performance analytics may also be recorded to optimize user interface responsiveness and systemic reliability.

Collected contact metrics and personal listings are utilized strictly to bridge direct peer-to-peer communication between clients and verified independent providers. NaijaFix maintains a strict policy against selling, leasing, commercializing, or transferring user database inventories to unauthorized external marketing entities.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: Does NaijaFix act as a direct employer, contractor, or financial escrow agent?
A: No. NaijaFix is solely a digital directory listing and marketing ecosystem. We do not employ artisans, supervise field projects, guarantee workmanship quality, or hold funds in escrow. All commercial contracts, agreements, project management, and financial settlements occur entirely between the client and the independent artisan.
Q: What criteria determine whether a professional receives a "Verified" badge?
A: Verified badges indicate that the artisan has successfully completed our baseline administrative identification checks, phone validation protocols, and category screening. However, this status does not constitute a formal corporate guarantee or insurance underwriting of their physical trade services.
Q: How do featured banner advertisements and sponsorships function?
A: Sponsored advertising spaces rotate dynamically at the prominent top header positions of both our Home page and Browse Directory page. Advertisers secure exposure slots for set durations, driving incoming client traffic directly to their dedicated business WhatsApp contact channels.
Q: Can listed professionals modify, update, or remove their business details?
A: Yes. Registered practitioners retain the right to request profile modifications, portfolio updates, or complete data expungement from our database archive by submitting a formal request through our administrative support desk.
Q: Does NaijaFix charge commission fees on completed jobs or transactions?
A:Yes. Taskly applies a standard service commission or percentage deduction on transactions and job fees processed or facilitated through the ecosystem. This percentage covers platform maintenance, artisan verification protocols, dispute assistance, and operational infrastructure .
Q: What legal jurisdiction governs the operations and agreements of NaijaFix?
A: All terms of service, platform usage agreements, and dispute resolutions are interpreted and enforced in accordance with applicable local commercial laws and data privacy regulations.
Q: How are platform errors, bug reports, or technical suggestions handled?
A: Technical feedback, interface anomalies, or platform suggestions can be submitted directly to our development and administrative support desk via our official WhatsApp reporting channels.
